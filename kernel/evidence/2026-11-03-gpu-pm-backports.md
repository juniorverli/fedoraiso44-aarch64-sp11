## Relevance to the reset

**The OPP UAF does not match this device's observed resume path.** Hamoa selects chip `0x43050c01` (`hamoa.dtsi:3990-3991`), whose catalog entry selects `a7xx_gpu_funcs` (`a6xx_catalog.c:1573-1585`). That table uses `a6xx_gmu_pm_resume` (`a6xx_gpu.c:2866-2873`), not `a6xx_pm_resume` in the wrapper table (`a6xx_gpu.c:2832-2839`). Thus the UAF and PM-error fixes are valid upstream fixes to another path, but the 66 ms runtime-idle setting does not establish that they ran on this GPU. Do not call the UAF the primary cause of this reset.

The GPU OPP owner and secondary-rail corrections do affect GMU RPMh vote construction (`a6xx_gmu.c:1710-1778,1783-1871`). Incorrect dependency votes could plausibly cause power instability during GPU operation or power transitions, and the 66 ms idle period makes transitions frequent; neither the actual vote values nor a causal link to the silent reset have been established. The two observed GMU starts and missing trace are documented in `plan/rev20.md`; they are not proof of either fault. Device validation remains required.
