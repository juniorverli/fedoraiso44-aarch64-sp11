The udev pin can run after the first in-kernel DRM open, so the "trace after the pin" predicate is invalid; judge by timestamp versus first autosuspend/post-pin GMU start. `hardware/gpu/test-plan.md` and `hardware/gpu/README.md` still carry the superseded dual-device/GMU-write/pmsg/ramoops text.
