## 1. Digital Microphones (`audio-mics`)

### Root Cause
In M14, the DMIC0/DMIC1 multiplexers and capture mixers were configured cleanly (`VA DMIC MUX0=1`, `VA DMIC MUX1=2`, `VA_AIF1_CAP Mixer DEC0/1=on`, `VA_DEC0/1 Volume=100`).
In M15, commit `f9d532d` introduced `SP11-Routes.conf` which included `SP11-MicGain.conf` within `SectionDevice."Mic"`. Because `SP11-MicGain.conf` defined an `EnableSequence` with only volume controls, ALSA UCM2 replaced the `EnableSequence` defined by `DMIC0EnableSeq.conf` and `DMIC1EnableSeq.conf`. Consequently, `VA DEC0/1 MUX`, `VA DMIC MUX0/1`, and `VA_AIF1_CAP Mixer DEC0/1` remained unprogrammed at `0` (`ZERO`) and `off`. Both direct ALSA capture (`arecord`) and PipeWire recorded digital silence.

### Fix
- Updated `SP11-MicGain.conf` to declare the full `EnableSequence` (routing `VA DEC0/1 MUX` to `VA_DMIC`, `VA DMIC MUX0/1` to `DMIC0`/`DMIC1`, `VA_AIF1_CAP Mixer DEC0/1` to `1`, and `VA_DEC0/1 Volume` to `98` = +14 dB) and `DisableSequence`.
- Added the DMIC routing defaults directly into `SectionVerb` in `Surface11-HiFi.conf` (in both `overlay` and `overlay-speakers`) so the hardware is always initialized when the card verb is opened.
- Exported standard `PATH` in `audio-routes.sh` to ensure `wpctl` is found.
- Verified offline with `mic-gain-check.sh` and `ucm-routes-test.sh` in the Fedora 44 container (ALL PASS). Committed in `worker/audio-mics` and merged to `main`.

## 2. Speaker Audio (`audio-speakers` & `kernel`)

### Root Cause
In boot `a238af95` (`sp11.entry=spk`), the speaker guard was armed in gated mode (`open_allowed: yes, allowed: no`), which keeps the speakers muted until `/usr/libexec/sp11/speaker-first-test` is run as root.
When the feedback path prepared, `q6apm-lpass-dais` threw:
`q6apm-lpass-dais: Error: Invalid channels (4)! Failed to set media format -22` on `WSA_CODEC_DMA_TX_0`.
In `sound/soc/qcom/x1e80100.c` (patch 0015), `x1e80100_be_hw_params_fixup` set `channels->min = channels->max = 4`. However, `audioreach.c` (`audioreach_speaker_protection_vi`) expects `num_channels <= 2` because `num_channels` represents the speaker count (2 speakers), and internally AudioReach doubles it (`num_channels * 2 = 4`) for V-sense and I-sense mappings.

### Fix
- Updated patch 0015 (`0015-ASoC-qcom-x1e80100-gate-speakers-on-the-speaker-prot.patch`) to set `channels->min = 2; channels->max = 2;` for `WSA_CODEC_DMA_TX_0`.
- Bumped `series_rev` to 13 in `kernel/base.toml` and recalculated `series_sha256`.
- Verified `kernel_series_check: PASS`. Committed in `worker/audio-speakers` and `worker/kernel` and merged to `main`.
