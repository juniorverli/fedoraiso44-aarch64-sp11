## Shutdown unit default

Recommendation: do NOT ship `sp11-ssam-shutdown.service` active by default. It is an untested hypothesis (H1), it duplicates what the kernel `.shutdown` already sends, it adds a bounded delay risk and a removal path that leaves the hub device behind (finding 3), and an always-on unit removes the baseline that the operator sequence in `2026-10-30-keyboard-reboot.md` section 6 needs (step 1, no mitigation). The image worker tree now gates it with `ConditionKernelCommandLine=sp11.ssam_shutdown` (the script checks again), like the rebind unit, with the ordering fixed. Both units stay preset-enabled and skipped without their argument; `check-m11-payload.py` asserts it. The change is uncommitted in the image worktree and needs a new ISO to take effect: the M11 ISO already built still has the unit active.
