## 2. Inference

- I1 (from F2, F3): the Apps-to-CDSP direction worked for at least the first two messages. The CDSP only opens channels after it has both a VERSION_ACK for its own VERSION, so it read our VERSION_ACK. The DT mailbox client and signal id are therefore correct; hypothesis (a) in its simple form (wrong IPCC client id) is killed, and DT differences vs ADSP/CRD are absent (F8).
- I2 (from F3, F4, F6): after about 4.45 s the CDSP stops consuming or answering Apps messages, and also sends nothing spontaneous for 160 s. The failure starts roughly 70-80 ms after the last message it acted on, in both rounds (IPCRTR at 4.5 s, fastrpc at 27 s). That excludes a slow-probe race on the Apps side.
- I3: the cause is on the CDSP side or in the Apps-to-CDSP wake path, not in the Linux GLINK state machine, which is identical for the ADSP edge (F5).
- Remaining candidates, not separable yet:
  - C1 CDSP firmware is gated on a step Windows performs (Windows has extra SMP2P events QB_RDY and DS_ACK, `2026-10-29-rea-windows.md` section 6.1) and stops servicing GLINK until it happens. Hypothesis.
  - C2 CDSP firmware idles or power-collapses after boot and the IPCC signal does not wake it (F10 hint, weak). Hypothesis.
  - C3 CDSP firmware stalled or crashed without raising wdog/fatal (F1, F7 make this unlikely). Hypothesis.
- No patch is established: nothing in the DT, driver or log shows a wrong value, and a change would be a guess. Firmware identity (SKU or signing) is excluded as a cause of silence: the image authenticated, reached `ready`, negotiated GLINK and announced channels.

## 3. Decision

No fix patch. Diagnostic patch `patches/0002-rpmsg-glink-log-fifo-indices-on-open-ack-timeout.patch` added: on the OPEN_ACK timeout it logs the head and tail words of both SMEM FIFOs (`sp11-glink: FIFO tx head=N tail=N rx head=N tail=N`). Meaning: `tx tail == tx head` means the CDSP read our OPEN and stayed silent (C1/C3-like: firmware gating); `tx tail != tx head` means it never read it (C2: wake or doorbell, or stalled firmware). Checked: `checkpatch --strict` clean (no Signed-off-by by design), `git apply --check` on v7.3 plus the full current `kernel/series` (which includes 0001) clean. Not built or run.

## 4. Next diagnostics (exact)

1. Kernel: append 0002 after 0001 in `kernel/series` (request R-K3). One more boot of the same entry; read the new `FIFO` lines in dmesg for IPCRTR (about 10 s) and fastrpc (about 32 s). Fastrpc (late) tells whether the tx tail ever advanced after boot.
2. Cmdline, same boot: add `trace_event=:mod:qcom_glink,smp2p:*,remoteproc:*` (the `smp2p` and `remoteproc` events exist in 7.3: `smp2p_update_bits`, `smp2p_notify_in`, `smp2p_negotiate`, `rproc_qcom_event`). Gives the CDSP `slave-kernel` bit values per edge and our `master-kernel` writes; Windows waits on three ready bits plus a DS ack (see C1), Linux uses four IRQs.
3. Collector (read-only): for both remoteprocs `cat /sys/kernel/debug/remoteproc/remoteprocN/{resource_table,carveout_memories}` and `trace0` if present (firmware log buffer, may show why the CDSP stops); `qcom_stats/cdsp` and `adsp` read twice 5 s apart; `/proc/interrupts` glink line deltas.
4. One operator-run manual test (not a collector action), only after the above, ADSP untouched: in the booted ISO, after login, `echo stop > /sys/class/remoteproc/remoteproc1/state; sleep 2; echo start > ...`, then `dmesg | grep sp11-glink`. If the CDSP then acks, the cause is boot ordering or timing against ADSP/pd-mapper (C1 or C2); if not, firmware or an Apps step Windows adds. CDSP carries no charging or battery function; it is reversible by reboot.
5. REA (owner of that tool): decode the Windows `qcsubsys8380.sys` SMP2P Apps-to-subsystem entry writes (QB, DS ack) and whether Windows toggles any `master-kernel` bit besides stop. A Linux write of those bits would be the candidate patch if I3 plus I1 and the FIFO result point to C1.
