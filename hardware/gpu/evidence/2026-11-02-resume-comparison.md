## Minimal discriminator and markers

Request image owner to make an opt-in debug-only service set `/sys/bus/platform/devices/3d00000.gpu/power/control` to `on` **before the first GPU runtime autosuspend**, retain it throughout the session, and log write result, readback, `runtime_status`, and monotonically timestamped service start/end. Verify actual ordering against the first `GPU_BOOT_TRACE` start and all later starts; a late service that permits earlier suspend/resume invalidates a clean no-resume comparison. GPU and GMU are different devices: keep the GPU platform device on, not just `3d6a000.gmu`. Do not assume sysfs write succeeds or that GMU internal IFPC is disabled. Keep the matching kernel, firmware, DT, GNOME workload and safety/thermal gates; safe remains default. Capture durable kmsg, boot ID, actual cmdline, booted firmware version and hash, power-control/status and reset reason if available.

**Valid test:** power/control is `on`, GPU status active, and after initial startup no additional GMU firmware-start marker occurs, including across the same GNOME use and observation window. A second marker makes the proposed explanation and test setup inconclusive (possible earlier race or independent GMU restart); investigate ordering and status. A sample alone cannot prove no brief transition: retain full marker stream. Avoid a new stress test until power-thermal releases it.

- Survives substantially beyond the prior reset window under matched use: **Inference**, repeated runtime suspend/resume or effects of holding the device active are implicated; this does not identify a defective line, prove PM alone, or eliminate altered power/clock behavior. Repeat matched control and measure markers before patching.
- Still resets with the no-repeat-start evidence: **Inference**, the specific repeated GPU platform runtime-resume trigger is weakened; investigate GPU power, clocks, firmware/IFPC, workload/VM and other concurrent causes. It does not rule out initial resume or internal GPU power collapse.
- Service fails, starts late, logs are incomplete, workload is unmatched, or outcome varies: inconclusive.
