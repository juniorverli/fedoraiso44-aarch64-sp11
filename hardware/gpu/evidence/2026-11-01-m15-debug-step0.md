## Observations

The GPU probe binds at 5.088580 s. At 5.197632 s, the DRM driver logs `Direct firmware load for qcom/gen70500_sqe.fw failed with error -2`; at 5.197636 s it reports `adreno_request_fw` failed to load `gen70500_sqe.fw`. The initrd policy in `image/kiwi/overlay/root/etc/dracut.conf.d/90-sp11.conf` explicitly excludes `gen70500_*`, assuming `msm_open()` first runs only after root is mounted. The L0 initrd artifact at `out/image-work/l0-sp11-fedora44-aarch64-kiwi-20261031T204236Z-private/iso-tree/boot/aarch64/loader/initrd` is zstd compressed (SHA-256 `47d7b90e12ca2d7161f5b8da27a1ada9a94e6ee636a54fa070f958ab107b7657`). `lsinitrd` is unavailable in this environment, so its member list was not independently re-read in this session; the omission is established from the dracut config, not inferred from `-ENOENT` alone.

Linux 7.3 source establishes the call path: `drivers/gpu/drm/msm/msm_drv.c:266-273` (`msm_open` calls `load_gpu`); `adreno_device.c:68-96` (`adreno_load_gpu` immediately calls `adreno_load_fw` and returns on error); `adreno_gpu.c:536-613` (`adreno_request_fw` tries `qcom/` and legacy direct paths, then helper fallback, and returns `-ENOENT` if all fail). Therefore the logged error proves an early DRM file open reached firmware loading before the GPU diagnostic service began at 29.462 s. The failed `adreno_load_gpu()` leaves `priv->gpu` unset (`msm_drv.c:198-209`), so a later open may retry.

This particular failed attempt returns before runtime PM is enabled (`adreno_device.c:108-120`), and therefore before GMU resume, GMU firmware/HFI startup, or `msm_gpu_hw_init()`/zap PAS. The available log has no later successful firmware-load line and no `GPU_BOOT_TRACE` markers; however, because the diagnostic state ends at `STEP0_END` (29.88 s) and does not record an armed marker or `STEP1_BEGIN`, the log cannot rule out a subsequent attempt or establish why the hook transition stopped. The `step0.pm` snapshot reports runtime status `unsupported`, and `step0.genpd` reports GPU CX/GX domains off. These are consistent with no completed GPU hardware initialization captured by the collector, not proof that no earlier or later open was attempted.

The firmware error is not a generic harmless probe miss: it is a concrete missing-at-that-time file lookup in the initramfs path, by design of the current image config. The root filesystem later contains compressed `gen70500_sqe.fw.xz` and `gen70500_gmu.bin.xz`, and the built kernel config has `CONFIG_FW_LOADER_COMPRESS_XZ=y`; those later facts do not establish availability at 5.197 s. Whether the early open is triggered by a particular userspace process and whether the early miss alone explains the boot/reset remain unknown.

## Kernel diagnostic change

A default-off `CONFIG_DRM_MSM_GPU_BOOT_TRACE` patch adds low-volume markers around the lazy `adreno_load_gpu()` stages and inside `a6xx_gmu_resume()` around GMU firmware/HFI start. It does not change firmware lookup, power, clocks, frequencies, or thermal behavior. Markers around hardware init are emitted outside `gpu->lock`. With the option enabled, a controlled boot can distinguish firmware/ucode failure, runtime-resume/GMU failure, and later hardware-init failure. It does not isolate zap/PAS within `msm_gpu_hw_init()` and must be combined with the collector and image hook markers. Enabling it may slightly affect timing; it is intended only for an explicitly controlled diagnostic build.

## Limits and next evidence

No successful GPU runtime resume, GMU start, zap/PAS, render session, or actual reset phase is present in this capture. The root cause is undetermined. The next image must mark immediately before and after the GPU hook and make the complete helper operation time-bounded; its current `flock` timeout does not bound mount/sync/unmount. The image owner must also stage the `gen70500_*` firmware in the initramfs and verify GDM/DRM open ordering. This must not be enabled in a test ISO until early opens are isolated from uncontrolled GPU initialization and the operator's thermal safety conditions are satisfied: shipping firmware earlier may convert a failed open into actual GMU/zap startup before the guard. Never enable an accelerated default boot for this diagnosis.

The two touched ARM64 objects (`adreno_device.o`, `a6xx_gmu.o`) compiled with `CONFIG_DRM_MSM_GPU_BOOT_TRACE=m` and again with the option disabled, using `aarch64-linux-gnu-gcc`. `strings` found phase-marker strings only in the enabled objects. `checkpatch.pl --strict --no-tree` reports zero errors/warnings, and `git apply --check` against the Linux 7.3 base passes. This is an object-level cross-compile only: the full kernel/BTF build and KUnit/device execution were not performed here.
