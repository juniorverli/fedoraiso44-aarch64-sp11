## UCM failure and gain

- **Verified:** S `journal-warnings.txt.gz`/`journal.txt.gz` show WirePlumber `spa.alsa: Failed to enable UCM device Speaker` at 44.004584 and 116.523836 s, but do not identify the cset or errno. S `extra-audio.txt.gz` hashes match canonical `image/component-inputs/d28/audio/Surface11-SpeakerSeq.conf` SHA-256 `73526c093294589554cad1326a46e2acaa7b4a83394b66af3048cca614312879` and `Surface11-HiFi.conf` `ba24e854477ce3ed0fc13c9dda6a7bb0bcf2d978f0fff4e672a6f879bdb6a90c`. `x1e80100.conf:35-43` selects SP11 by DMI; `Surface11-HiFi.conf:20-38` includes the speaker sequence and sets `PlaybackMixer` to `default:${CardId}` / `Speakers`. That playback mixer is distinct from the sequence control handle.
- **Verified:** The Fedora UCM package's `codecs/wsa884x/two-speakers/init.conf:2-8` (image extraction `out/image-work/l0-sp11-fedora44-aarch64-kiwi-20261103T011453Z-private/ucm-test/ucm2/`) defines `Speakers Volume` as an ALSA **userspace remap** of the two mono PA controls. The packaged `lib/ctl-remap.conf:6-16` creates a `default` remap over `hw`. The Linux codec defines each PA control, not the aggregate (`sources/linux/sound/soc/codecs/wsa884x.c:1732-1751`). Thus `amixer -c 0` showing the alias is not proof it exists on `hw:0`.
- **Verified from upstream ALSA 1.2.16 source:** [`src/ucm/main.c:739-806,956-971`](https://github.com/alsa-project/alsa-lib/blob/v1.2.16/src/ucm/main.c#L739) resolves sequence csets to `PlaybackCTL`/`CaptureCTL`, auto-filled as `hw:<card-id>` unless explicitly overridden, and aborts on a negative cset. `PlaybackMixer` does not select that cset handle. The local UCM does not override PlaybackCTL. Canonical `Surface11-SpeakerSeq.conf:16-18` writes left PA 6, right PA 6, then `Speakers Volume` 6. Therefore **Inference, strong but not traced:** the final alias cset on `hw` returns ENOENT, after both physical PA writes; that is the leading explanation for each Speaker enable failure. Another earlier cset or device-specific ALSA configuration/error could be responsible; only a controlled trace with cset and errno confirms it. Do not claim the snapshot proves the ordering of the actual failure.
- **Verified:** S `extra-audio.txt.gz` reports both PAs 6, alias 6,6, DAC/COMP/BOOST/PBR on, VISENSE/CPS off, MODE Speaker, RX0/RX1 and RX0_MIX at 72 (−12 dB per reported TLV); macro RX muxes select AIF1_PB and INP0 select RX0/RX1. The file omits RX1_MIX content due to collector truncation; do not assume it. Caps ready and all six late-probe cap return values are in S `dmesg.txt.gz`. The mixer snapshot shows the desired PA and main routing at that time, not the gain during the reported listening interval or proof that UCM considered the device enabled. Because failure is after PA writes if the hypothesis holds, the failed sequence alone does not imply PA was left at 0; partial state or later changes remain possible.
- **Verified:** `image/overlay/usr/libexec/sp11-audio-safety` mutes and verifies mute, then requests 5% only with `sp11.audio_first_test=1`, otherwise 100%; S `cmdline.txt.gz` has no first-test marker, and the helper exited successfully at 44.167 s in `journal.txt.gz`. **Inference:** it executed the 100% branch; this does not measure subsequent sink/stream volume, mute state, effective software gain, whether it chose the actual output, or the audible level. ## Interrupt and shutdown

- **Verified:** S `journal.txt.gz` records a Reboot request at 135.605767 s and `The system will reboot now!` at 135.614256 s. S `sound.txt.gz` reports device :1 interrupt `0x00 0x02 (PA off)` at 136.087892 s, latch at 136.091328 s, and :0 the same status at 136.199451 s. WirePlumber's service enters stop-sigterm at 136.555215 s in the journal. Thus this happened during reboot teardown, before the logged WP stop; the actual inducing transition and whether either PA had previously been on are not established. Journal and kernel collection use slightly different renderings; times above use the monotonic timestamps of each record.
- **Verified:** candidate/integrated `out/rev21-apply/sound/soc/codecs/wsa884x.c:1724-1747`, also `hardware/audio-speakers/patches/0013-ASoC-codecs-wsa884x-add-V-I-sense-and-CPS-capture-an.patch:217-248`, reads status0/status1, clears them, applies masks, obtains the driver's `pa_on` flag, and deliberately latches every enabled nonzero initialized interrupt, including PA off. `(0x02 << 8) | 0x00` is `0x200`. Read/clear return values are not checked there. The cited driver labels the bits undocumented; PA off is a driver state, not proof of the physical fault condition. The guard is then latched until reboot (`patches/0012...:355-376`). S `extra-audio.txt.gz` shows `fault: 1`, open_allowed/allowed no, `mode: open-loop`, `caps_ready: 1`. S1 safety and the prior explicit idle-fault review (`evidence/2026-10-31-flash-review-verdicts.md`, F-05) rule out ignoring unknown bits just because the PA is off.
- **Hypothesis:** a shared shutdown/clock/PA transition produces a non-damaging interrupt on both devices. It is temporally plausible, not decoded or established. Real supply/clock, protection or other fault remains possible. Identify STATUS1 bit 1 from authoritative register definitions or catalogued, properly attributed Windows analysis, then observe it across idle, start/stop and shutdown with read-error/timing data. Do not mask, discard, clear the latch in userspace, bypass unmute gates, or suppress all idle faults. A kernel diagnostic proposal should retain fail-closed behavior on status/clear I/O errors and distinguish PA state and shutdown timing; no functional kernel patch is justified yet. The observed shutdown latch cannot explain low output earlier in this boot, though a latch during active use would block later output.

Playback reported working after the S events is not proof that errors are harmless.

**Verified:** S `clk_summary.txt.gz` snapshot lists WSA core TX MCLK with enable count 1 and SoundWire iface `mclk` enable count 0 at 9.6 MHz; D1/D2 show the iface count 1 at their respective snapshots. S `regulator_summary.txt.gz` shows both amp consumers with active use counts under `vreg_l12b_1p2` and `vreg_l15b_1p8`; DT supply associations are `sources/linux/arch/arm64/boot/dts/qcom/x1-microsoft-denali.dtsi:1092-1105`. Snapshots do not establish clock or rail state at 44 s, actual measured voltage, or causality. **Hypothesis:** transient startup or session transition noise; cannot yet call it benign. Monitor timestamped recurrence during muted setup, idle and the permitted test; persistent/repeated parity, correlated data errors or faults stop the test and require investigation, not clock/voltage changes on this evidence.

Capture actual PipeWire sink and per-stream levels, mute, selected route, application input and hardware state during playback; software volume percentages and per-stream attenuation are distinct. Do not assume 100% at startup remained in effect, or raise the existing RX/MIX initialization 72 merely because cap 81 exists. A verified persistent low result at those bounds needs more evidence, not a higher cap, changed mode/PBR, disabling the latch, or a closed-loop claim. No guarantee of a particular loudness is established.
