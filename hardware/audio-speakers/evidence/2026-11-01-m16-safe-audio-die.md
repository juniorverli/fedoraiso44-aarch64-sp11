# M16 safe audio and the reported DIE

## Verdict and evidence limits

**TBD:** The cause of the DIE is unknown. Audio is a plausible boot-time exposure, not a demonstrated cause; this review did not identify a specific DT-induced kernel oops that can be fixed responsibly. The D26 speaker path is **not established safe for the fallback**: S1's D26 exception explicitly requires a speaker-off fallback, and neither its probe nor amplifier settings have device validation. Recommend removing the speaker fragments from m15-safe and retaining microphones/DP for the initial fallback; see the exact request below. This is containment and policy compliance, not proof of an audio cause. Do not open an audible speaker stream to diagnose the crash.

**Verified, operator-transcribed incident only:** On Surface Pro 11, M16 private ISO, kernel `7.3.0-65.sp11.14.fc44`, a DIE (taint D) occurred before 46.66 s; the visible end of its call trace was a kworker. At 47.72 s a WARN at `drivers/gpu/drm/drm_vblank.c:1329` reported `msm_dpu ae01000.display-controller: [drm] vblank wait timed out on crtc 0`, in `drm_fb_helper_damage_work`; operator reports the WARN repeats. Listed modules include `soundcore`, `msm`, `panel_edp`, `i2c_qcom_cci(+)`, `camcc_x1e80100`. The oops header, fault address, PC/LR, complete backtrace and preceding boot log were not transcribed. No original photograph or raw journal is available here. **Inference:** the later display warning is separately identified; its relationship to the earlier DIE is unknown. A loaded module list is not a call trace, and neither `soundcore` nor `i2c_qcom_cci(+)` proves causation.

**Verified:** M15.1 safe and Spectra captured boots had disabled swr0 and no WSA link, sound card/VA/DP present, no reported oops or vblank warning, and two WSA macro flat-cache warnings; see [M15.1 evidence](2026-11-01-m15.1-audio-safe-boots.md). They do not isolate M16 changes or prove all audio paths safe. The M16 source enables speakers and adds DP and closed-loop fragments; the operator identifies image commit `2ee4dbf` as applying speaker UCM on safe/debug. The current image source conditions `sp11-speaker-ucm.service` on `sp11.entry=spk`, `debug` or `safe` (`image/overlay/usr/lib/systemd/system/sp11-speaker-ucm.service:3-5`; `image/overlay/usr/libexec/sp11/speaker-ucm-enable:7-18`). Whether the service ran on the incident boot, the precise route and whether userspace opened any stream at the DIE are **TBD**.

**Artifact:** published record is `out/dtb-variants/7.3.0-65.sp11.14.fc44.aarch64/qcom/x1p64100-microsoft-denali-m15-safe.json` (the requested `.dtb.json` name does not exist), SHA-256 `91d5136b24bb213c9ab10c650c64fb462df2277c758e39ae3d18567dbea75173`; DTB SHA-256 `b8b9f43071ffba3a3d3f810b8f26a2218b2bade097834d9b3e507a5f5d78084d`. Record says series rev 14, source archive v7.3 plus release patches, and reports swr0, children and sound enabled. Driver references below use base `sources/linux` or `out/linux/m15` as the available integrated source for the patch series; the DTB record names `out/linux/m15` but its build used an archive. Running module binary/source equivalence and the booted DTB hash remain **TBD**.

## Static review

- **Verified — GPIO cells:** record lines 4229–4232 and 4289–4292 show `<0xe4 0x0c 0x01>` and `<0xe4 0x0d 0x01>`. `0xe4` is the phandle of `lpass_tlmm`, not GPIO 228: the record's symbolic diff lines 6384–6406 and `out/linux/m15/arch/arm64/boot/dts/qcom/x1-microsoft-denali-audio-closedloop.dtsi:20-45` resolve offsets 12/13, active low. `sources/linux/arch/arm64/boot/dts/qcom/hamoa.dtsi:4636-4648` defines two GPIO cells and 23 GPIO ranges; `sources/linux/drivers/pinctrl/qcom/pinctrl-sm8550-lpass-lpi.c:57-80,197-209` defines pins 0–22. **Inference:** neither index is out of range. Electrical assignment and correctness of the two reset lines remain **TBD**; in-range is not physical validation.
- **Verified — supplies and master:** board DTS `out/linux/m15/arch/arm64/boot/dts/qcom/x1-microsoft-denali.dtsi:1391-1423` names `vdd-1p8` and `vdd-io`, reset and six port mappings for each WSA. `sources/linux/arch/arm64/boot/dts/qcom/hamoa.dtsi:4548-4584` supplies swr0's `0x06b10000` MMIO resource, `iface` clock, interrupt, reset and port arrays. `out/linux/m15/drivers/soundwire/qcom.c:1550-1688` maps the platform resource, checks mapping/clock/IRQ/config errors and initializes the master; it does not use a parent regmap on this platform. It does not check the return of `clk_prepare_enable` or every register read. `out/linux/m15/sound/soc/codecs/wsa884x.c:2274-2378` checks regulator/reset acquisition and SoundWire regmap creation, validates battery cells and starts cache-only before enumeration. **Inference:** no missing required supply/clock or wrong regmap base is demonstrated by the DT; inaccessible MMIO/power or timing faults are not ruled out by static checks. The reset and regulator references do not prove voltage, physical wiring, enumeration or successful I/O.
- **Verified — WSA/mixer and gate:** D26 adds `status = "okay"` and a tooling marker (patches 0003, 0008); 0016 also adds amplifier properties and WSA playback/VI/CPS links. Attach and cached PA initialization occur in `out/linux/m15/sound/soc/codecs/wsa884x.c:1621-1690`; card limits are installed by `out/linux/m15/sound/soc/qcom/x1e80100.c:36-59`. The checked integrated source has a closed-loop guard, not a driver that interprets the D26 marker as an open-loop enable: `sp11-guard.c:64-100,250-285` checks fault, data, graph, VI and calibration; `wsa884x.c:2005-2031` enforces it at unmute. `sp11.nospk` acts at that gate, not at controller/codec probe. **Inference:** a muted or refused stream cannot rule out a probe-time fault; marker/UCM and volume caps do not establish protection. Whether the shipped module matches this source and whether a stream was started are TBD.
- **Verified — ADSP/APR:** `sources/linux/drivers/remoteproc/qcom_q6v5_pas.c:1909` matches X1E ADSP; ADSP provides services beyond audio, so disabling its remoteproc is not a safe audio isolation. In the integrated source patches 0006/0007 publish initialized q6apm state and install GPR callback before probe (`out/linux/m15/drivers/soc/qcom/apr.c:364-382`, `sound/soc/qcom/qdsp6/q6apm.c:868-907,924-940`). Card component probe loads and parses topology (`q6apm.c:848-851`, `topology.c:1415-1447`); patches 0014/0018 add graph/config operations, and 0018's generic configuration is reached via media-format setup (`audioreach.c:929-981,1598-1683`). **Inference:** even with speakers off, APM callbacks and topology parsing can run; graph configuration requires the relevant graph/stream path, which is not established from the fragment. No crash is established in these paths.
- **Verified — microphones:** the microphone fragment enables the card, replaces routing with VA DMIC0/1 and removes WSA playback; it also includes DP (`out/linux/m15/arch/arm64/boot/dts/qcom/x1-microsoft-denali-audio-mics.dtsi:19-28`). The board sets `vdd-micb`, DMIC pin state and 2.4 MHz (`x1-microsoft-denali.dtsi:1176-1182`); `sources/linux/sound/soc/codecs/lpass-va-macro.c:169,1440-1482,1544-1603` validates 9.6 MHz / 2.4 MHz = 4 and checks clock/MMIO/regmap errors. **Inference:** this value is a supported divisor, not an out-of-range cause. Signal, actual power/clock and capture operation remain TBD; M15.1 only showed an endpoint.
- **Verified — DP:** patch 0017 adds two DAI links to already-described DP0/1 and q6apm, without directly modifying DRM vblank code (`out/linux/m15/arch/arm64/boot/dts/qcom/x1-microsoft-denali-audio-dp.dtsi:17-49`). Machine probe parses links/registers the card (`x1e80100.c:278-325`). **Inference:** card/component binding and DP audio can interact at runtime, but the later vblank warning alone does not identify a DP-audio fault. The safe panel is eDP, while the links name external DP0/1; topology and component binding still need device evidence.
- **Verified — scope of patches 0001–0018:** 0001/0004 PA gain, 0002/0005 mixer limits, 0003/0008 DT opt-ins, 0006/0007 APR ordering, 0009 initial gate superseded by 0015, 0010/0011 WSA properties, 0012 guard, 0013 WSA VI/fault handling, 0014 graph support, 0015 machine gate, 0016 DT, 0017 DP links, 0018 generic graph parameters. Presence in the series is not proof a particular execution path ran. No patch is proposed without a faulting instruction and reproduction.

## Isolation and confirmation plan

Collect the **complete first** DIE header, exception type, PC/LR, registers, all call frames, workqueue name, modules, timestamps and logs before and after; use persistent collector/journal or complete sequential photographs if storage is unavailable. Record boot ID, command line, exact ISO/kernel/DTB hashes, actual DT and UCM/PCM state. If boot is unstable, use already-approved recovery and collect without playing audio. Do not treat a single successful boot as proof.

| Grade and hypothesis | Controlled device observation that would confirm or weaken it |
|:--|:--|
| **Hypothesis H1: WSA/SoundWire probe or attach causes DIE.** | Same M16 image/kernel/arguments and camera/display DT, compare requested speaker-off safe DTS below against original, repeatedly from comparable cold boots. Verify effective swr0 status and WSA bind/enumeration, timestamps, full oops. Recurrence only with swr0 and a PC/stack in its probe/attach is supporting evidence; occurrence with swr0 disabled weakens H1. Keep speakers muted; no playback. |
| **Hypothesis H2: sound card/VA or AudioReach/APR topology causes DIE.** | If H1-off still fails, compare same speaker-off DT with `/sound` disabled as a separate diagnostic while leaving ADSP and all non-audio nodes intact. Capture card/APM/topology probe timing, full PC/stack, actual UCM and active PCM. An audio stack with reproducible sound-node dependence supports H2; persistence without the card weakens card-only causation, but independently probed LPASS/ADSP remains. |
| **Hypothesis H3: DP audio/card binding causes the fault or later vblank loop.** | On speaker-off card variant compare DP links present vs deleted, identical other DT and UCM, capture DRM state, display and complete oops; reproducible link-dependent failure with relevant PC/trace supports H3. No external DP playback; keep eDP recovery available. |
| **Hypothesis H4: UCM change starts or configures a problematic audio stream.** | On same speaker-off DT compare microphone-only UCM versus M16 speaker overlay, no manual playback; capture UCM service, WirePlumber/ALSA journal and PCM state before first fault. Association plus a matching trace supports it; no running stream and an earlier fault weaken stream-specific H4. UCM can change userspace controls, not execute in kernel itself. |
| **Hypothesis H5: unrelated camera/display/other work causes first DIE.** | Full fault PC/stack and matching targeted device logs, then isolate the named owner’s path with that owner; a later `drm_fb_helper_damage_work` warning or module list alone does not establish H5. |

A/B tests should be separate approved diagnostics, with a known-good recovery entry, bounded boot count and no changes to battery, firmware or speaker protection. A surviving boot or difference in timings alone cannot prove causation. Cross-check any new hardware value before `sw-ready` per protocol §5.1.

## Kernel and image requests

**Kernel:** replace the three includes in `kernel/dtb-variants/x1p64100-microsoft-denali-m15-safe.dts` as follows; retain all camera/display/USB overrides below. The mics fragment already includes DP, so do not include DP twice:

```diff
-#include "x1-microsoft-denali-audio-d26.dtsi"
-#include "x1-microsoft-denali-audio-dp.dtsi"
-#include "x1-microsoft-denali-audio-closedloop.dtsi"
+#include "x1-microsoft-denali-audio-mics.dtsi"
```

Adjust the header to describe microphones/DP and speakers off. Verify compiled effective swr0 is disabled, speaker children effectively disabled, `/sound` enabled, VA and DP links present, all WSA playback/VI/CPS links and the D26 marker absent; apply default safety profile and compare all unrelated nodes with M16 safe. This exact change is a proposal, not built or device tested here. Preserve a separate diagnostic for H1 only under D26's conditions and recovery plan; the fallback must remain speaker-off.

**Image:** use microphone-only UCM on speaker-off safe, update entry/profile checks, and capture the UCM service and active PCM state. Retain the D26 main-entry exception only as authorized by the operator and preserve a speaker-off fallback. This request does not claim UCM caused the DIE.

**Audio-mics / kernel:** assess VA/DMIC and collect endpoint plus a nonzero capture only after a stable boot; no microphone correction is justified by the missing oops. Investigate H2/H3 if full trace points there. Do not change ADSP PAS or disable ADSP charger services to bisect audio.
