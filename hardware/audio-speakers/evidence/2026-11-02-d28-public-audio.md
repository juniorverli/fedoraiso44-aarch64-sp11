# D28: public topology and open-loop default

Status: **designed, not ready for build or audible test**. Operator-provided reference and decision: `plan/decisions.md` D28; D26 and `plan/risks.md` S1 permit only the specified muted-by-default, capped open-loop exception. The reference's speakers and microphone work, but that is not a device test of this integration. The public graph has no SP, VI, CPS or SP diagnostic event; it does not provide thermal or excursion protection. The closed-loop experiment remains a separate, fail-closed opt-in.

The safety substitutions are required: upstream WSA macro initialization writes 84, above the kernel maximum 81, and upstream SpeakerSeq writes PA 12, above the 0 dB kernel maximum 6. The hardware caps (PA max 6, RX/MIX max 81, reattach restore and EAR SPKR constraint, patches 0001/0002/0004/0005) remain mandatory. Check failures of cap installation rather than assuming `snd_soc_limit_volume()` succeeded. No request here authorizes an increase in any cap.

## Kernel request: required semantics

No new kernel patch is supplied here: the kernel owner must implement and validate this contract before build. 0003 creates speaker fragments; 0008's `/chosen/sp11,speaker-policy = "d26-open-loop-v1"` is currently tooling-only; 0009 refuses playback, and 0015 replaces that unconditional refusal with 0012's guard. 0012 requires closed-loop loaded to open and graph/VI/CPS/calibration to unmute. 0016 adds feedback links and hardware settings intended only for the experiment. Neither the marker nor absence of a file currently selects open loop. `topology.c` calls `audioreach_sp11_load()` unconditionally after topology loading; that loader ignores missing supplements, but the guard still refuses. The diagnostic registration in 0014 occurs for an SP module; the public topology should never reach it. 0019–0021 are candidates outside the integrated series; do not use them as the D28 fix, and do not introduce the degraded closed-loop path without a separate reviewed decision.

Implement a latched, observable mode, established before opening or unmuting a WSA stream. Explicitly distinguish (1) Denali with exact D26 marker, designated public/open-loop DT and successfully parsed public topology confirmed to have no SP/VI/CPS module, from (2) explicitly marked closed-loop experiment and (3) all missing, unknown, contradictory, incomplete or invalid states. Open-loop permits WSA open/unmute only when its complete required prerequisites and D26 caps are established, with `sp11.nospk` and amplifier faults still prohibiting both. Do not require `sp11cl`, SP enable, diagnostics registration, VI/CPS or calibration there, and do not call/register SP events. An open-loop DT must include 0008's fragment and DP links but **not** 0016; make a new variant based on the current working safe/debug base, rather than reusing the existing `m13-spk` which includes 0016. Its explicit experiment marker must be absent. Build-time and runtime checks must reject an SP-containing, malformed or incomplete topology on that DT; mere failure to find/load the private supplement is never a reason to fall back to open loop.

Experiment DT must explicitly select closed-loop and include 0016; preserve all 0012/0013/0014/0015 graph, VI/CPS, calibration, fault, kill and amplifier-unmute requirements, with no downgrade on missing or rejected SP diagnostics. Invalid combinations, missing topology, missing mode or inconsistent markers deny playback. Address the asynchronous mute/stream lifecycle, reprobe and topology unload so no stale mode, no cross-card global state and no transition to open loop can occur while playing. Verify with tests for missing/bad marker, topology load failure, surprise SP in public graph, no supplement, experiment failures, kill/fault and teardown; assert `sp11_guard_check` and `sp11_guard_unmute_check` both use the correct mode. Avoid classifying based on absence observed before topology parsing is complete. Report mode and reason in debugfs. Retain the guard's amplifier-side enforcement, and independently verify PA/digital/MIX limits and error handling.

## Muted device test and exit criteria

Operator present for any audible test. First boot speaker-off fallback; verify no WSA speaker link/bus and mic/DP where applicable. On safe and debug, before opening audio verify actual DT policy/mode, public topology hash, `nospk` clear, no fault, initial software mute, PA/RX/MIX caps and actual UCM routes. Open the speaker PCM while muted, confirm successful prepare/start with no `-22`, no SP module diagnostics registration attempt (`APM_CMD_REGISTER_MODULE_EVENTS` for `0x0800138c`) and no corresponding DSP unsupported response; collect full kernel journal with timestamps and graph/module evidence. Absence of a log alone is not proof if registration lacks instrumentation: add a trace/counter or instrumented build that proves zero attempts. Record the guard mode and both open/unmute decisions. Confirm no sound while muted.

With operator present, make a single short low-level playback after deliberately unmuting: both speakers play at limited volume, no distortion or unexpected heat; stop on either. Capture a short microphone sample and verify actual signal, not merely a PCM open. Test both DP routes with a display connected before PipeWire starts. Collect mixer values, actual sink state, profile/routes, PCM and topology, and before/after journal. Separately verify `sp11.nospk`, faults, and the closed-loop experiment remain fail-closed without any audible test. A speaker playing or a clean prepare alone does not prove thermal protection.
